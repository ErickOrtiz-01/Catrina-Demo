THE CATRINA EXPERIENCE

A neighborhood cantina made for good food & good company.

La Catrina of Argenta brings colorful Mexican dining to Main Street with a menu built around shareable appetizers, taquería favorites, hearty plates, desserts, and refreshing aguas frescas.

Come for a quick lunch, settle in for dinner with friends, or make Catrina part of your next night out in Argenta.
